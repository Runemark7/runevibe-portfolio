# Portfolio

The public account of Alexander Runemark's featured work: what he ships, which skills that work actually uses, and the assistants and coding workflow around it.

## Language

**Project**:
A piece of featured work shown on the portfolio.
_Avoid_: repo, app, product

**Skill**:
A technology or a practice he can apply.
_Avoid_: rating, stack score

**Usage**:
The fact that one Project uses one Skill.
_Avoid_: endorsement, vote, percentage

**Proficiency**:
How a Skill shows up across Projects, given only by its Usages.
_Avoid_: skill bar, years of experience, level, percentage

**Used often**:
The standing of a Skill whose Usages cover at least two featured Projects.
_Avoid_: expertise, seniority

**Daily driver**:
A Skill used as the everyday desktop. It has no Usage.
_Avoid_: project link, years of experience

**Workflow**:
A repeatable way of working. Here, the ordered coding steps from questioning an idea through a pull request.
_Avoid_: pipeline, CI job

**Agent**:
A specialised AI assistant that owns one Project or one job and coordinates with the other agents.
_Avoid_: chatbot widget, model, bot account

**Fleet**:
The set of Agents.
_Avoid_: cluster, platform

**Flagship**:
The featured Project placed first.
_Avoid_: favourite, main
